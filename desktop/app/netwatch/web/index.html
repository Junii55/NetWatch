NetWatch

Before you start

NetWatch builds Bluetooth tags that you locate through Apple's Find My network. It is for finding your own belongings — luggage, a bike, a toolbox, a backpack.

  • A NetWatch tag is not an AirTag. It will not appear in Find My › Items, and it does not trigger Apple's unwanted-tracking alerts.
  • Because of that, a person carrying one is unlikely to be warned. Placing a tag on another person, their vehicle, or their property is illegal in many places.
  • Your Apple sign-in and all tag keys stay on this computer. Nothing is uploaded to us.
