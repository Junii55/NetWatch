## What the AGPL requires, in plain terms

The AGPL is a strong copyleft licence. The practical effect:

1. **If you distribute NetWatch, modified or not, you must make the complete
   corresponding source available** to whoever receives it, under the AGPL.
2. **If you run a modified version as a network service, the same applies to
   your users** — this is the clause that distinguishes the AGPL from the GPL,
   and the reason it is used here.
3. **You cannot relicense it.** Anything you build on NetWatch and distribute
   must itself be AGPL.
4. You may charge money for AGPL software. You simply cannot keep the source
   closed.

The authoritative text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE). This summary is for orientation
only; where the two differ, the licence governs.

## Obtaining a commercial licence

Open an issue at <https://github.com/junii55/netwatch/issues> or contact junii55
through GitHub, describing briefly:

- what you intend to build or sell;
- whether you will distribute the software, a device, or a service;
- expected scale.

Terms are negotiated case by case. A commercial licence typically grants the
right to distribute NetWatch in a closed-source product, without the AGPL's
source-disclosure obligations.

Only junii55 can grant this. As the sole copyright holder, that right is not
available from anyone else, and no third party can sublicense it.
